# Launch Plan Review

Reviews a product launch plan, judging whether the launch tier is named and resourced to match, every audience has a message and a moment, internal teams are enabled rather than surprised, execution is sequenced and owned, success is instrumented before launch day, and risk, rollback, and a slipping date are all planned for.

## How to use this skill

Apply the rubric in `rubric.md`, bundled in this folder, to the material the user provides. Follow the rubric exactly as written: its criteria, its scoring, its output format, and its honesty rules. Do not merge it with other rubrics, and do not soften or omit findings the rubric requires.

This rubric includes a Context requests section. Before judging, ask the user for that context in one grouped round, and only for items the rubric names. If the user cannot or does not want to answer, proceed and report the gap the way the rubric instructs.
