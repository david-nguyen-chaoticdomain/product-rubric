# Positioning Review

Reviews a positioning document against the five components of effective positioning, judging whether the competitive alternatives are the real ones, the unique attributes are actually unique, the value has proof, the best-fit customer is specific, and the market category is chosen rather than defaulted into.

## How to use this skill

Apply the rubric in `rubric.md`, bundled in this folder, to the material the user provides. Follow the rubric exactly as written: its criteria, its scoring, its output format, and its honesty rules. Do not merge it with other rubrics, and do not soften or omit findings the rubric requires.
