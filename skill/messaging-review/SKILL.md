# Messaging Review

Reviews a messaging document, judging whether it leads with an insight rather than the product, engages the alternatives honestly, lets the differentiated value carry the hierarchy, attaches proof at the right strength, keeps the customer as the subject, and answers objections before closing with a clear ask.

## How to use this skill

Apply the rubric in `rubric.md`, bundled in this folder, to the material the user provides. Follow the rubric exactly as written: its criteria, its scoring, its output format, and its honesty rules. Do not merge it with other rubrics, and do not soften or omit findings the rubric requires.
