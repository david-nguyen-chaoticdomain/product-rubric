# Battlecard Review

Reviews a competitive battlecard, judging whether its claims about the competitor are sourced and dated, whether the competitor's strengths are stated honestly, whether the card arms a rep for both winning and losing ground, whether it can be used in the middle of a live call, and whether its freshness is owned.

## How to use this skill

Apply the rubric in `rubric.md`, bundled in this folder, to the material the user provides. Follow the rubric exactly as written: its criteria, its scoring, its output format, and its honesty rules. Do not merge it with other rubrics, and do not soften or omit findings the rubric requires.
