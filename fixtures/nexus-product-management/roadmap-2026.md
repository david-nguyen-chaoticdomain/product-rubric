# Nexus Product Roadmap - Q4 2026 to Q2 2027 | Prepared by Marcus Webb

### How to Read This Roadmap

Now holds the work in progress this quarter. Next holds the problems we take on next, in order. Later holds direction beyond that, and is expected to change. Outcomes are what we are trying to make true; the initiative is how we currently expect to get there. This roadmap is shared with the whole company; a shorter customer version is published from it each quarter.
