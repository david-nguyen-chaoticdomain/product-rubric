# Fixture brief: Nexus product management documents

Four documents from the fictional Nexus product team, each written to be judged by one of the product management rubrics, and all about the same piece of work, Project Templates, so the documents can be read against each other:

- `prd-project-templates.md` — the requirements document for Project Templates (judged by **PRD Review**).
- `roadmap-2026.md` — the Nexus roadmap for Q4 2026 to Q2 2027, in Now, Next, Later form (judged by **Roadmap Review**).
- `problem-statement-admin-stall.md` — the problem statement the PRD acts on (judged by **Problem Statement Review**).
- `user-stories-project-templates.md` — eight user stories for the same feature (judged by **User Story Review**).

The fifth management rubric, Project Plan Review, is served by the timeline and risks inside `fixtures/nexus-website-launch/project-brief.md`.

Each document ships as Markdown and as a PDF in the same document style. Every document is realistic and mostly sound, and each contains deliberate weaknesses of the kind these documents actually ship with, listed in `expected-findings.md`. Nexus, its people, and its dates are fictional.
