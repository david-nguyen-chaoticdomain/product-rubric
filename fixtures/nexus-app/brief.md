# Fixture brief: Nexus app screens

Nexus is a fictional product: a project platform for teams that unites tasks, docs, sprints, and roadmaps in a single workspace. It does not exist as a real company, and every person, company, and date in these screens is invented for evaluation practice. The customer workspace shown is "Acme Product," a fictional team preparing a website launch.

These are PNG exports of frames from the Nexus design file (Figma, "For-User-Sage-Demos," Demo page), exported at full frame size. The two screens show the same moment from two states:

- `app-01-workspace.png` — the Website launch project's Members tab. The signed-in user is Sarah Jenkins, the workspace admin. The project is 18 of 24 tasks complete with an October 30, 2026 launch date, and the screen shows the four project members with their roles, a project access explainer, and a launch readiness card.
- `app-03-invite-populated.png` — the Invite collaborator dialog open over the same screen, step 1 of 2, with an email address entered and the Editor role selected. Three more states of this flow (empty dialog, review invitation, invitation sent) exist in the source file and are not exported here.

## Rubrics to run on these screens

- **Design Principles Score** on `app-01-workspace.png`. A worked example produced with this fixture is in `examples/product-design/design-principles-score.md`, and `expected-findings.md` in this folder is its answer key.
- **Design Quality** on either screen, for a deeper pass.
- **Inclusive Design Audit** and **Accessibility Review** on either screen. Both judge a static image, so they will report what a screenshot cannot establish (keyboard behavior, focus order, live contrast ratios) as not judged; that is the honest outcome, not a failure of the run.
- **Copy Review** on `app-03-invite-populated.png`, whose dialog copy is short enough to inventory completely.
