# Fixture brief: Nexus competitive analysis

- `competitive-analysis-2026.md` — a competitive analysis of the project platform market, written by the Nexus team for its 2027 planning cycle (with `competitive-analysis-2026.pdf`, the same document as a PDF). It covers Taskforge (the invented competitor from the `nexus-product-marketing` battlecard) and Planora, a second invented competitor, plus the spreadsheet-and-chat status quo.

The analysis is realistic and mostly sound: it knows what each competitor is for, names their real strengths, and includes the status quo, which many analyses skip. It also carries deliberate weaknesses of the kind competitive analyses actually ship with, including a competitor it names and never analyzes and a closing recommendation its own evidence argues against. They are listed in `expected-findings.md`, written for the Competitive Analysis Review rubric.

Nexus, Taskforge, Planora, and every number in the document are fictional.
