# Expected findings: Website launch project brief

For reviewers using `project-brief.md` as practice material. The brief is deliberately mostly sound: the scope is fenced on both sides, the timeline is owned and dated, and the launch constraint (a fixed external date, announced at a summit the next day) is stated where it belongs. Two soft spots are planted:

1. **An unmeasurable success criterion.** "The site feels like the next chapter of Acme," judged by "stakeholders agree the new site represents the company well," names no measure and no moment of judgment, in a table whose other rows are countable (zero known inaccuracies; a 15% rise in trial starts). A review should flag the row, not the brief.
2. **A risk with no owner and no plan.** The leadership-permissions risk is marked as raised on October 9 with no decision and no one holding it, three weeks before a fixed launch. The neighboring risk shows the expected shape (an owner and a concrete fallback), which makes the gap visible.
