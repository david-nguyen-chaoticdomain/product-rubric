# Website Launch - Project Brief | Prepared by Sarah Jenkins

### Summary

Acme Product is replacing its marketing website. The current site predates the company's current product line, describes features that have moved or been retired, and sends trials to a signup flow the growth team no longer uses. This brief covers the relaunch: new homepage, product pages, pricing page, and docs landing, on the existing domain, launching October 30, 2026. The date is fixed: the launch is announced in the CEO's keynote at the Acme customer summit on October 31, and the site has to be live the day before.

### Background

The website is the company's highest-traffic surface and its least maintained one. Content has been edited in place for three years by whoever needed a change, so the product story differs between the homepage, the pricing page, and the docs. Support tickets regularly cite website claims the product no longer keeps. The project exists to give the company one accurate, current account of what Acme does, in one place, before the summit puts new attention on it.

### Objectives

|Goal|Success Criteria|
|---|---|
|One accurate product story across the site|Every product claim on the new site is checked against the current product before launch; zero known inaccuracies at launch|
|More trial signups from the homepage|Trial starts from homepage traffic rise at least 15% in the first month against the October baseline|
|The site feels like the next chapter of Acme|Stakeholders agree the new site represents the company well|

### Scope

In scope:

- Homepage, product pages, pricing page, and docs landing, written and designed new
- Navigation and footer for the whole marketing site
- The trial signup entry points on those pages, handed to the growth team's current flow

Out of scope:

- The docs themselves (content moves as is; rewriting is a later project)
- The blog and its archive
- Any change to the product or the in-product signup flow

### Audience

Prospective customers evaluating Acme against two named competitors, current customers checking what their plan includes, and summit attendees who will visit the site from the keynote announcement. The site is written for the first group first.
