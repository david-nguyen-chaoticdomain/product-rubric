# Fixture brief: Nexus website launch project brief

- `project-brief.md` — the project brief for the Website launch project at the fictional Acme Product (with `project-brief.pdf`, the same document as a PDF). It is the same project whose workspace screen appears in the `nexus-app` fixture: same owner (Sarah Jenkins), same team, same October 30, 2026 launch date, same 18 of 24 tasks complete. The brief is written in the document style of the study plans in `nexus-study-plans`, so a team can see one format family across research and delivery documents.

The brief is realistic and mostly sound, with two deliberate soft spots a good review should find, listed in `expected-findings.md`. It is input material, not a rubric target of its own: the questions it feeds are the ones a team would extract from it.

## Rubrics to run with this document

- **Problem Statement Review**: extract the problem statement the brief is acting on (it is implied by the Background, never stated as one) and run the rubric on the extraction. The gap between the two is the lesson.
- **Project Plan Review**: the timeline, owners, and risks sections form a small plan on their own; the fixed external launch date is its hard constraint.
- **PRD Review**: as a contrast case. A brief is not a PRD, and the rubric's Missing grades for requirements detail show exactly where brief ends and specification begins.
