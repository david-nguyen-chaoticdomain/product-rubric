# Nexus Onboarding - Survey Test Plan | Prepared by Jordan Lee

### Summary

This plan covers a survey of new Nexus workspace admins at day 30 after signup. The study exists to learn where new admins stall in their first month, so the team can decide which onboarding fixes to build next and whether the invite flow needs a guided first run. The survey is run by the Nexus product research team, and its readout feeds the onboarding roadmap review in November 2026.

### Timeline

|Preparation (plan created)|October 12, 2026|
|---|---|
|Live (date study goes live)|November 3 to November 17, 2026|
|Readout (data analysis, findings report)|November 13, 2026|

### Objectives

|Goal|Success Criteria|
|---|---|
|Learn where new admins stall during their first month|At least 200 completed responses, with stall points ranked by frequency, so the team can pick the top three fixes|
|Judge whether the invite flow is understandable to new admins|Responses split cleanly between admins whose first invitation was accepted and admins whose was not, so the two groups' setup ratings can be compared|
|Decide whether the onboarding email sequence helps or annoys|The team feels confident about the email sequence after the readout|

### Hypotheses

- Admins who invite a teammate in their first week will report higher satisfaction at day 30 than admins who work alone.
- The setup checklist is the onboarding aid new admins use most.
- New admins find the pricing page confusing, and it slows down their setup.

### Participants

Workspace admins whose Nexus trial or subscription began thirty days before the send date, including admins who created a workspace but have not yet started a project. Invitations go to the account owner's email.

|Psychographic|Region|Age|Gender|
|---|---|---|---|
|Team leads and operations-minded organizers who set up tools for their teams|North America and Europe|25 to 54|All|

### Methodology

The survey is emailed to the day-30 cohort on the live date, with one reminder after seven days. It has fourteen closed questions and two open questions and takes about six minutes. Questions use the formats in the table below. Responses are stored in the research repository, reported in aggregate, and no individual response is quoted with a name attached.

### Questions

|Question|Possible Answer(s)/Format|To Determine|
|---|---|---|
|How would you rate your overall experience with Nexus so far?|Single answer: Very satisfied / Satisfied / Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied / Dissatisfied / Very dissatisfied|An indicator of how satisfied people are with their experience on the product|
|How much did you enjoy using Nexus in your first month?|Single answer: A lot / A little / Not at all|How positive the first month felt|
|Was setting up your workspace fast and easy to understand?|Single answer: Yes / No|Whether setup reads as quick and clear|
|In your first month, which of these did you use?|Multiple answer: Setup checklist / Templates / Invite a teammate / Automations / None of these|Which onboarding aids admins actually touch|
|How many teammates have you invited to your workspace?|Single answer: 0 / 1 to 2 / 3 to 5 / 6 or more|Whether the admin has crossed the invite step at all|
|How fair do you think Nexus pricing is for a team your size?|Single answer: Very fair / Somewhat fair / Neither fair nor unfair / Somewhat unfair / Very unfair|Whether pricing expectations set by the pricing page match the first month's experience|
|What nearly stopped you from finishing setup?|Open text|The stall points the closed questions did not anticipate|
