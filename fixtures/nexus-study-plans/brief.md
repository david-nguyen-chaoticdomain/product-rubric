# Fixture brief: Nexus study plans

Two study plans for the fictional Nexus product, written in the test plan formats this project's maintainer uses in his own research practice (a survey test plan and a usability study test plan), and filled in for Nexus studies:

- `survey-test-plan.md` — a day-30 onboarding survey of new Nexus workspace admins (with `survey-test-plan.pdf`, the same document as a PDF).
- `usability-test-plan.md` — a moderated usability study of the Nexus invite flow, the same flow shown in the `nexus-app` fixture screens (with `usability-test-plan.pdf`).

Nexus, its people, and its dates are fictional. Both plans are realistic and mostly sound, and both contain deliberate weaknesses of the kind study plans actually ship with: a readout scheduled before data collection ends, a success criterion that is not a criterion, a hypothesis the instrument never tests, participants who cannot answer the questions asked of them, a task that gives away the interface it is meant to test. `expected-findings.md` in this folder is the answer key, written for the Study Plan Review rubric.

## Rubrics to run on these plans

- **Study Plan Review** on either plan. It is a deep rubric: it will ask for context the plan does not carry (the decision the study feeds is stated in each plan's summary; anything else it needs, it should name as missing rather than assume).
- The plans also serve as style references: teams writing their own study plans in these formats will find the Study Plan Review judges them against the same sections.
