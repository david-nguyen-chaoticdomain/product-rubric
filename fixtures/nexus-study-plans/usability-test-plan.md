# Nexus Invite Flow - Usability Study Test Plan | Prepared by Jordan Lee

# Summary

This plan covers a moderated usability study of the Nexus invite flow: the path a workspace admin takes to invite a teammate to a project, set their role, and confirm the invitation was sent. The study uses the current high-fidelity prototype of the flow (workspace screen, invite dialog, review step, confirmation). Findings go to the product team before the flow ships, with a decision attached: ship as is, or fix the blockers the study finds first.

# Objectives

|Goal|Success Criteria|
|---|---|
|A first-time admin can complete the invite without moderator help|At least 4 of 5 participants send the invitation unaided|
|Participants give the invited teammate the role the scenario asks for|Observe behavior during the role task|
|A participant can tell, afterward, whether the invitation was actually sent|At least 4 of 5 participants state the invitation's status correctly when asked immediately after the final task|

# Hypotheses

- Participants will look for inviting under the Members tab before they try the button in the project header.
- Participants will understand the three roles (Project admin, Editor, Viewer) from the picker alone, without opening the permissions help.
- The review step will catch at least one wrong role or wrong address across the five sessions.

# Participants

Five participants who lead or coordinate a team project at work and have set up a shared tool for their team before. A mix of current Nexus admins and people who use a competing project tool, so the flow is judged on first use rather than on habit.

# Methodology Screening Questions

1. Do you currently lead or coordinate a team project at work? (Must answer yes)
2. Have you set up a shared workspace or project tool for a team in the past year? (Must answer yes)
3. Have you sent at least one invitation from inside Nexus before? (Must answer yes)
4. Which project tools do you use weekly? (Open answer, used for the customer and non-customer mix)

# Methodology

Moderated remote sessions over video call, thirty minutes each, one facilitator and one note taker. Each participant works through the five tasks below in the prototype, thinking aloud, then answers the post-study questions, including the desirability word exercise. Sessions are recorded with consent, and each participant receives a $50 gift card.

# Tasks

1. You have just been asked to bring your teammate Maya onto the Website launch project so she can help with the launch. Get her added to the project.
2. Click Invite collaborator and send an invitation to maya.chen@acmeproduct.com.
3. Before the invitation goes out, change Maya's role so she can view the project and leave comments, but cannot edit tasks.
4. You sent the invitation a moment ago. Check whether it went through, and find where you would resend it if Maya says she never got it.
5. A member of the project has left the company. Remove Daniel Kim from the Website launch project.
