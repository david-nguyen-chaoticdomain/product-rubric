# Nexus Positioning | Prepared by Tessa Grant

### Positioning Statement

For product teams who ship on a weekly rhythm, Nexus is the project platform that keeps tasks, docs, sprints, and roadmaps in one workspace, so the plan and the work stop living in different tools. Unlike stitching together a task app, a docs app, and a spreadsheet, Nexus carries one project from brief to launch with its context intact.

### Competitive Alternatives

What customers would do if Nexus did not exist:

- Keep projects in spreadsheets and status docs, and coordinate in chat threads
- Use a general to-do app for tasks and a separate wiki for documents
- Stay with the legacy project suite their company bought a decade ago and work around it

### Unique Attributes

- Tasks, docs, sprints, and roadmaps in one workspace, sharing the same projects and permissions
- Sprint cycles that roll over automatically, with cycle metrics computed from the board itself
- Roadmap items tied directly to the tasks that deliver them, so a date on the roadmap is a fact about work, not a wish
- Kanban boards with drag and drop

### Value and Proof

|Unique Attribute|Value for the Customer|Proof|
|---|---|---|
|One workspace for tasks, docs, sprints, and roadmaps|Status meetings shrink, because the answer to "where are we" is on the screen|Customers tell us Nexus cuts status meetings in half|
|Sprint cycles that roll over automatically|Teams stop spending Friday afternoon on sprint bookkeeping|Cycle setup takes one click; metrics come from the board|
|Roadmap tied to tasks|Leads commit to dates they can defend|The Website launch project at Acme Product shipped against a roadmap date set six weeks earlier|

### Target Market Characteristics

Teams of 2 to 2,000 people, in any industry, who run projects and want one tool for the work. Best fit so far: software product teams of 20 to 200 who ship weekly and feel the seams between their task tool, their docs, and their roadmap.

### Market Category

Project platform: the category for tools that carry a project end to end (plan, work, track, report) rather than one slice of it.
