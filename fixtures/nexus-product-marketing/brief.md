# Fixture brief: Nexus product marketing documents

Four documents from the fictional Nexus marketing team, one for each product marketing rubric:

- `positioning.md` — Nexus's positioning, in the five components (competitive alternatives, unique attributes, value and proof, target market characteristics, market category) (judged by **Positioning Review**).
- `messaging-sprints-2.md` — the messaging and sales pitch for the Sprints 2.0 launch (judged by **Messaging Review**).
- `launch-plan-sprints-2.md` — the launch plan for Sprints 2.0, launching November 18, 2026, the same release the homepage in `nexus-homepage` announces (judged by **Launch Plan Review**).
- `battlecard-taskforge.md` — a sales battlecard against Taskforge, an invented competitor (judged by **Battlecard Review**).

Each document ships as Markdown and as a PDF in the same document style. Every document is realistic and mostly sound, and each carries deliberate weaknesses of the kind these documents actually ship with, listed in `expected-findings.md`. Nexus, Taskforge, their people, prices, and dates are fictional.
