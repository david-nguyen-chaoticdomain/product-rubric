# Nexus Sprints 2.0 - Launch Plan | Prepared by Tessa Grant

### What Is Launching

Sprints 2.0: automatic cycle rollover, cycle metrics computed from the board, velocity trends, and a new sprint planning view. It ships to all workspaces on November 18, 2026. The positioning and messaging for the launch live in their own documents (Nexus Positioning; Nexus Sprints 2.0 - Messaging and Sales Pitch).

### Launch Tier

Tier 1. Sprints are the reason most teams choose Nexus, and 2.0 is the release the sales team has been promising since summer. The company treats this as a market moment, not a feature drop.

### Success Measures

- Sprint feature adoption rises 25% in the first month after launch.
- At least 40% of active sprint teams enable automatic rollover by December 18.
- Launch email click rate above the lifecycle average.

### Risks

- If the feature freeze slips, launch day moves with it; the homepage pill and the email are date-flexible.
- Support expects questions about rollover rules in the first week; the help article is part of launch assets.
