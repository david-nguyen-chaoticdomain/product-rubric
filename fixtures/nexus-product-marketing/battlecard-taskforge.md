# Battlecard: Nexus vs Taskforge | Nexus Sales Team

Last updated: October 2026. Owner: (unassigned)

### Competitor Overview

Taskforge is a task-first project tool sold to small and midsize teams. It is strong on price and simplicity: a clean board, quick setup, and a loyal base among teams under 20 people. It added docs in 2025 and reports in 2026. Most Taskforge deals we see are teams that have outgrown it: the work has spread across boards, docs live elsewhere, and status reporting has become a manual Friday job.

### How to Spot Them

- The prospect says "we mostly live in Taskforge, but docs are in a wiki and the roadmap is a spreadsheet."
- Boards are described as "fine," but reporting is "a Friday project."
- The team has just hired past 25 people, or a second team wants in.

### Questions to Ask

- "When leadership asks where a launch stands, what do you open?"
- "Where does a decision made in a doc show up in the plan?"
- "How long does sprint or cycle reporting take, and who builds it?"

### Landmines to Lay

- Ask what happens to a roadmap date when the underlying tasks slip. In Taskforge the roadmap is a drawing; in Nexus it is computed from the work.
- Ask who maintains their status deck, and what that hour costs every week.

### Objection Handling

|Objection|Response|
|---|---|
|"Taskforge is cheaper."|Compare totals, not stickers: their reporting tier and per-seat docs add-on close most of the gap, and the Friday status hour is a cost too.|
|"Our team likes Taskforge's simplicity."|Nexus starts from the same board. The difference appears when the second team, the docs, and the roadmap arrive.|
|"Taskforge is adding roadmaps soon."|Their roadmap has been "coming" since the 2024 acquisition, and development has stalled since. Judge what you can buy today.|

### Why We Win

- One workspace: tasks, docs, sprints, and roadmaps share projects and permissions, so status is a screen, not a meeting.
- Sprints run themselves in 2.0: rollover and cycle metrics computed from the board.
- Migration from Taskforge is an import, not a project: boards, tasks, and docs come over in one pass.
