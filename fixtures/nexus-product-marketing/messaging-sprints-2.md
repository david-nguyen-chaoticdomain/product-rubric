# Nexus Sprints 2.0 - Messaging and Sales Pitch | Prepared by Tessa Grant

### The Pitch

Nexus Sprints 2.0 is our biggest sprint release yet. It adds automatic cycle rollover, cycle metrics computed from the board, velocity trends across cycles, and a new sprint planning view. Sprints 2.0 makes Nexus the fastest way to run a sprint: set the cycle length once, and Nexus moves unfinished work forward, closes the cycle, and opens the next one with its metrics ready. No other project platform automates the whole cycle.

### Key Messages

|Audience|Message|Proof|
|---|---|---|
|Team leads running sprints|Your sprint runs itself between planning and retro|Automatic rollover and board-computed cycle metrics, new in 2.0|
|Product managers|The roadmap and the sprint finally agree|Roadmap items tied to the tasks that deliver them|
|Workspace admins|Nothing to configure per team|Cycle settings inherit from the workspace|

### The Story

Every week, a team lead somewhere loses an hour to sprint bookkeeping: closing the old cycle, carrying work over, rebuilding the same report. She did not become a lead to move tickets between columns. Then Nexus arrives with Sprints 2.0, rolls the cycle over on its own, computes the metrics from the board, and hands her the hour back, every week, forever. Her roadmap updates itself from the same work. The team just ships.

|Objection|Response|
