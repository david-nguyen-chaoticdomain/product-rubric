# Fixture brief: Nexus research reports

- `findings-invite-flow.md` — the findings report for the Nexus invite flow usability study, the same study planned in `fixtures/nexus-study-plans/usability-test-plan.md`: five sessions, October 19 to 23, 2026, report dated to the plan's readout. It ships as Markdown and as a PDF in the same document style.

The report is realistic and mostly sound: it counts its evidence, quotes participants by ID, separates findings from recommendations (mostly), and carries an honest limits note. It also contains deliberate weaknesses of the kind findings reports actually ship with, including two findings that contradict the report's own appendix and its own summary. They are listed in `expected-findings.md`, written for the Findings Review rubric and its claim audit.

## Rubrics to run with this document

- **Findings Review**: the report's claims are auditable against its own appendix, which is where two of its findings fail.
