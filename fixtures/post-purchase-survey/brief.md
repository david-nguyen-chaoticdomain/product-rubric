# Fixture: post-purchase survey

A fictional survey, written for this repository as test material for the Survey Review rubric (`rubrics/product-research/survey-review.md`). The worked example produced from it lives at `examples/product-research/survey-review.md`.

## The fiction

Ledgerline is a fictional bookkeeping app for freelancers. It does not exist. This questionnaire is the draft its fictional team wrote to send to new customers thirty days after signup.

- **Purpose (as the team stated it):** understand the first-month experience, so the team can decide what to fix in onboarding and whether the pricing page is setting the right expectations.
- **Audience:** customers who signed up about thirty days ago, on any plan, including people still in their trial.
- **State of the draft:** written quickly by the team, never reviewed. It is about to be sent to roughly 2,000 customers.

## How to use this fixture

Run the Survey Review rubric against `questionnaire.md`, with the purpose and audience above as the supplied context. Then compare the result with `expected-findings.md`, the answer key: every flaw in this questionnaire was planted deliberately, and the key lists each one with the rating the rubric's criteria should produce.

A good run also leaves the sound questions alone. Two of the twelve questions are written correctly, and flagging them is a miss, not thoroughness.
