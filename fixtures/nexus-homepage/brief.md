# Fixture brief: Nexus marketing site

Nexus is a fictional product: a project platform for teams that unites tasks, docs, sprints, and roadmaps in a single workspace. It does not exist as a real company, and every name, claim, and price on these pages is invented for evaluation practice.

These are PNG exports of frames from the Nexus design file (Figma, "For-User-Sage-Demos," Demo page):

- `homepage-a-above-fold.png` — the marketing homepage, Option A (light theme), above the fold: navigation, hero, product showcase, and the first feature stories.
- `homepage-b-above-fold.png` — the same page, Option B (dark theme). Same structure and copy; the difference is the visual direction, which makes the pair useful for comparative runs.
- `homepage-mobile.png` — the homepage composed for a phone (390 px wide): stacked hero, both calls to action, product visual, and the features introduction.
- `pricing-page.png` — the pricing page in the dark direction: three plans (Starter $0, Pro $12 per member per month billed annually, Enterprise custom), a monthly and annual toggle, and a short questions section.

One deliberate edit: the design's fictional "trusted by" band, which named real companies as if they were customers, has been removed from these public exports. Nexus is invented, and the band could be misread out of context as a claim about real companies. The full-length homepage frames, with the band and the sections below the fold, remain in the source file for anyone working there.

## Rubrics to run on these pages

- **Five Second Test** on `homepage-b-above-fold.png` or `homepage-mobile.png`: what a visitor takes away from the hero in five seconds.
- **Attention Prediction** on either homepage: where a first-time visitor's eye is predicted to go, against the path the page intends (announcement, headline, primary call to action, product visual).
- **Copy Review** on `pricing-page.png`: a small, complete inventory of pricing copy, including the plan descriptions and the questions section.
- **Design Principles Score** or **Design Quality** on either homepage direction, or run against both to compare A and B on the same standard.
