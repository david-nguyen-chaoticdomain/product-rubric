## Subhead

- **Type**: paragraph

- **Suggested**: Keep as written.

## Highest impact fixes

1. **The subhead's version history claim contradicts the Starter card.** One of the two sentences is wrong, and a pricing page is the last place a buyer should have to arbitrate between a company's own statements. Fix the subhead, as above.
2. **The footnote promises Enterprise a trial the page does not offer.** "Every paid plan" is one word too broad, and it is the word an enterprise buyer will quote back.
3. **"Seats" and "members" are used as if they were the same word, in a page whose price depends on the difference.** Settle on "member" everywhere, starting with the Starter card.
